#### Dynamic Pricing in E-Commerce Industry using Multi-Armed Bandit Theory & Hierarchical Models  

## Project Overview

My project focuses on developing a dynamic pricing model that integrates Multi-Armed Bandit (MAB) techniques and hierarchical modeling. The aim is to establish adaptive pricing strategies that effectively address a variety of business scenarios, including:

* Maximize Revenue: Enhancing pricing strategies to optimize revenue without losing competitive edge.
* Increase Sales Volume: Adjusting prices to boost sales, particularly useful during promotions or high-demand periods.
* Clear Old Inventory: Strategically lowering prices to manage and reduce excess stock efficiently.
* Enhance Customer Satisfaction: Developing fair pricing policies that foster customer loyalty and enhance satisfaction.

## Methodology

My approach combines sophisticated hierarchical modeling with the flexibility of Multi-Armed Bandit algorithms, enhanced further by customer segmentation and demand forecasting:

* Hierarchical Modeling: This technique structures pricing strategies to respond to various levels of market and customer data. It adapts to factors such as geographic location, customer demographics, or product categories, providing customized pricing approaches.
* Multi-Armed Bandit (MAB): I use MAB within the hierarchical framework to dynamically explore and optimize pricing strategies, balancing the act of exploiting known profitable strategies with the exploration of potentially more advantageous ones.
* Customer Segmentation: To better tailor our pricing strategies, I segment customers based on their behavior, preferences, and demographic profiles. This allows for more targeted and effective pricing that can meet diverse customer needs and enhance engagement.
* Demand Forecasting: By predicting future demand trends based on historical data and market analysis, I can adjust pricing strategies proactively. This forecasting helps ensure that our pricing decisions are aligned with expected market conditions, balancing supply and demand effectively.
